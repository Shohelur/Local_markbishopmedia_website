# Phase 4 — Product Strategy

## Purpose
Translate business strategy into a clear product direction, including vision,
goals, MVP definition, and prioritization framework.

## Role
Product Strategist · Product Manager

## Inputs Required
- Phase 3: Business Strategy Document

## AI Actions

### Step 1: Product Vision
Write a clear, inspiring product vision statement:
- What will the product be in 3-5 years?
- What world does it create for users?
- Who will it serve?

### Step 2: Product Goals
Define measurable product goals:
- Launch goals (Phase 1 / MVP)
- 6-month goals
- 12-month goals

### Step 3: MVP Definition
Define the Minimum Viable Product scope:
- What is the smallest version that delivers real value?
- What is excluded from MVP?
- Why are excluded items out of MVP?

### Step 4: Feature Prioritization Framework
Use a simple prioritization model (e.g., MoSCoW):
- Must Have (MVP)
- Should Have (next version)
- Could Have (future)
- Won't Have (explicitly out of scope)

### Step 5: Product Roadmap (High Level)
- Phase 1 / MVP scope
- Phase 2 scope
- Phase 3+ scope

### Step 6: Produce Product Strategy Document
Create `docs/product-strategy.md`.
