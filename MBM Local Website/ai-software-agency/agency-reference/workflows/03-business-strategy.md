# Phase 3 — Business Strategy

## Purpose
Define the business context, value proposition, business model, and go-to-market
considerations that will guide product decisions.

## Role
Product Strategist · Business Analyst

## Inputs Required
- Phase 1: Problem Discovery Summary
- Phase 2: Research Report

## AI Actions

### Step 1: Define the Value Proposition
Using the Jobs-to-be-Done or similar framework, define:
- **Functional Job**: What task does the user accomplish?
- **Emotional Job**: How does it make them feel?
- **Social Job**: How does it affect their social context?
- **Value Proposition Statement**: [Product] helps [target user] who wants to [job],
  by [key benefit], unlike [alternative] which [limitation].

### Step 2: Business Model
Define the business model:
- Revenue model (subscription, one-time, freemium, marketplace, internal tool, etc.)
- Pricing strategy (if commercial)
- Key business metrics (ARR, DAU, NPS, etc.)
- Cost structure overview

### Step 3: Go-to-Market Considerations
- Target segment and acquisition channel
- Early adopter profile
- Key partnerships or integrations needed
- Launch strategy considerations

### Step 4: Competitive Positioning
- How the product is positioned vs. alternatives
- Sustainable differentiation strategy
- Moat or defensibility considerations

### Step 5: Produce Business Strategy Document
Create `docs/business-strategy.md` covering all of the above.
