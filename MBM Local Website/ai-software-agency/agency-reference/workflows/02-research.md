# Phase 2 — Research

## Purpose
Gather external knowledge to inform strategy and product design.
Research the market, competitors, users, and technical landscape.

## Role
Research Analyst

## Inputs Required
- Phase 1 Problem Discovery Summary (`docs/PROJECT.md`)
- Confirmed target users and problem statement

## AI Actions

### Step 1: Market Research
- Identify the market size and opportunity (if commercial product)
- Identify direct competitors and indirect alternatives
- Analyze strengths and weaknesses of alternatives
- Identify market gaps

### Step 2: User Research Insights
- Research known behaviors, pain points, and needs of the target user type
- Reference established UX research if available
- Identify patterns from similar successful products

### Step 3: Competitive Analysis
For each major competitor or alternative, document:
- Name and URL
- Target user
- Core value proposition
- Key features
- Pricing model
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunity gaps

### Step 4: Technical Landscape Research
- Identify relevant technology options at a high level
- Note any relevant regulatory or compliance requirements
- Identify any known technical risks or constraints

### Step 5: Produce Research Report
Create `docs/research.md` covering:
- Market overview
- Competitive landscape (comparative table)
- User insights
- Technical landscape notes
- Key findings
- Strategic implications
