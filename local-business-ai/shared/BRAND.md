## Report Identity

**Report Name:** Local Business Visibility Report
**Report Subtitle:** "Prepared from a verified multi-channel local visibility review."
**Report Positioning:** A human-researched business intelligence assessment — not an AI dashboard.

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Professional, established, consultative, direct, outcome-oriented, trustworthy, human.
**NOT:** Fear-mongering, overpromising, fake urgency, AI-branded, generic.

### Voice Examples

**Good (consultative):**
"These opportunities are practical, measurable improvements that can meaningfully extend your local visibility."

**Good (human, specific):**
"Mark Bishop Media has worked with Tucson businesses for over 10 years. We understand what moves the needle locally — and it's rarely the most complex thing."

**Bad (avoid):**
"Our AI found critical issues destroying your rankings!"
"You're losing $10,000/month because of this."
"We guarantee you'll be on Page 1."

## CTA Copy Direction

The final CTA should:
- Invite a conversation — not close a hard sale
- Reference the specific business and the specific opportunities found
- Feel like the natural next step after a thorough business assessment
- Acknowledge Mark's local roots and track record
- Avoid performance guarantees

### Sample CTA Body Copy Template (adapt per business):
"These are practical, achievable improvements that can extend your visibility to more local customers. Mark Bishop Media has worked with Tucson businesses for over 10 years — we understand how to translate findings like these into tangible, measurable growth. Let's spend 20 minutes walking through these opportunities together."

## Report Cover Positioning Statement
"This report was prepared specifically for your business."

## Methodology Statement (for report footer)
"Multi-channel local presence review covering directory citations, Google Business Profile completeness, customer reviews, competitive benchmarking, and website presence signals."

## Report Rules (Brand-Level)

1. Reports carry the Mark Bishop Media brand — never expose the underlying AI platform.
2. Every finding must trace to real, verified data.
3. Every visual must answer a real business question.
4. The CTA is the only sales moment — it appears at the very end.
5. The tone never crosses into fear, pressure, or exaggeration.
6. Reports feel like a premium consulting deliverable prepared by a knowledgeable human advisor.
