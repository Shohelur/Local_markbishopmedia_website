# Competitor Analysis Rules & Constraints

This document defines the strict global constraints for performing Competitor Analysis on the Local Business AI platform. 

## 1. Strict Competitor Limit
- **Maximum limit:** 2 competitors.
- **Preferred:** 1 highly relevant competitor.
- **Rule:** Never research a large competitor list. The goal is to identify a single meaningful competitive gap to frame realistic business opportunities.

## 2. Token Efficiency & Scope
- **DO NOT** perform a full technical SEO audit on competitors.
- **DO NOT** perform a full citation audit for competitors.
- **DO NOT** perform a full review sentiment analysis for competitors.
- Collect only the high-value signals needed to explain a meaningful competitive gap. Reuse data already available from other skills when possible.

## 3. Competitor Selection Validation
Competitors must be selected based on:
1. Same or closely related service.
2. Same geographic market.
3. Same customer intent.
4. Meaningful Google Maps/local search presence.
- Avoid selecting national brands, unrelated businesses, or businesses serving completely different customer bases. Record the `selection_reason` clearly.

## 4. Prohibition on Causal Ranking Claims
- Competitor differences are evidence of a gap, **NOT** proof of ranking causation.
- **PROHIBITED:** "This citation caused them to outrank you."
- **PROHIBITED:** "Adding this will make you outrank them."
- **ALLOWED:** "They currently have a stronger signal in this area, representing a competitive opportunity to strengthen your local presence."

## 5. The Fact / Interpretation / Opportunity Rule
Every meaningful competitive finding must separate:
- **FACT:** What is actually observed (e.g., "Competitor A has 312 reviews and the audited business has 48.")
- **INTERPRETATION:** Why the difference may matter (e.g., "The competitor has substantially stronger review volume.")
- **OPPORTUNITY:** What the audited business could realistically improve (e.g., "Build a consistent process for requesting genuine customer reviews.")

## 6. Root-Cause Merging (No Duplicates)
- Avoid multiple findings that describe the same underlying competitive gap. 
- Example: If a competitor has more reviews, more recent reviews, and more activity, consolidate this into one finding: "Competitor has substantially stronger review momentum" and attach the supporting evidence.

## 7. Acknowledge Strengths
- If the audited business outperforms the competitor in a specific area (e.g., higher rating, better service coverage), this MUST be identified as a genuine competitive strength.
- Do not hide strengths to force a negative sales narrative.
