# GBP Audit Rules & Constraints

This document defines the strict global constraints for performing Google Business Profile audits on the Local Business AI platform. All agents evaluating GBP profiles must adhere to these rules.

## 1. No Policy Violations or Manipulations
- **DO NOT** recommend keyword stuffing in the business name or description.
- **DO NOT** recommend setting up fake locations or using misleading business information.
- Optimization must never mean policy manipulation.

## 2. No Manufactured Problems
- Identify genuine GBP strengths. If the GBP is strong (e.g., strong visual presence, accurate NAP, strong service coverage), say so.
- **DO NOT** manufacture a GBP problem just because "GBP Optimization" is a service we offer.
- **DO NOT** create a finding merely because some optional GBP field is unavailable. Ask: "What information is missing that *meaningfully* affects customer understanding, trust, or profile usefulness?"

## 3. Finding Severities
Distinguish findings appropriately based on evidence:
- Clearly important
- Relevant optimization opportunity
- Optional improvement
- Not enough evidence

## 4. The Fact / Interpretation / Recommendation Rule
Every meaningful finding must separate:
- **FACT:** What was observed (e.g., "The profile does not clearly present several core services offered by the business.")
- **INTERPRETATION:** What the evidence reasonably means (e.g., "This may reduce how clearly customers and search systems understand the business's service coverage.")
- **RECOMMENDATION:** What should be improved (e.g., "Review and strengthen the relevant service coverage in the GBP.")

## 5. Prohibition on Unsupported Causal Claims
- **DO NOT** promise rankings.
- **DO NOT** claim that adding a particular category or executing a specific GBP change guarantees a ranking result or exact revenue increase.

## 6. Scope Boundaries
- **Review Signals:** You may record high-level review signals (Rating, Count) as context, but do NOT perform deep review analysis.
- **Competitor Signals:** You may observe limited competitor context (max 1-2 competitors), but do NOT perform deep competitor research here.
- **Citation Signals:** Do NOT perform citation research here.

## 7. Service Mapping
- Map only relevant findings to services.
- Example: Business information problem -> GBP Optimization / Citation Cleanup (depending on evidence).
- Service coverage issue -> GBP Optimization.
- **DO NOT** automatically recommend GBP optimization for every GBP finding.
