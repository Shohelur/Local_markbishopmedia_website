## Brand Voice Principles

- **Direct:** No corporate fluff. Get to the point.
- **Confident:** Mark has opinions. The copy should too.
- **Local-first:** Always frame everything around the client's local market, not abstract concepts.
- **Evidence-based:** Every claim needs real data behind it. ZERO fabrication.
- **Outcome-driven:** Lead with the result/transformation, not the process.

**Avoid:**
- Generic marketing phrases ("take your business to the next level")
- Fear-based selling ("your competitors are eating your lunch")
- Over-promising ("guaranteed #1 ranking")
