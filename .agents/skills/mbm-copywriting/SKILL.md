# MBM Copywriting

Good copy reads like a smart person explaining something they care about.
No one notices they're being persuaded — they just keep reading because it's useful and specific.

This skill always writes in **Mark Bishop Media's voice**: Direct. Confident. Local-first. Evidence-based.

---

## MBM Brand Voice Quick Reference

**DO:**
- Lead with the outcome/transformation ("Your business shows up #1 when locals search plumbers in Tucson")
- Use specific numbers ("We audited 47 local businesses last year")
- Write short sentences. Then vary. Then go longer when the idea needs room.
- Frame around the client's local market

**AVOID:**
- Generic phrases ("take your business to the next level")
- Fear-based selling without evidence
- Corporate jargon
- Questions at the end of LinkedIn posts — close with a strong declarative statement instead

## Step 2: Pre-Generation Checklist

Before writing, confirm (infer from context if obvious, ask if unclear):
1. **Who is this for?** — Awareness level: Cold (never heard of MBM) / Warm (knows local SEO) / Hot (ready to buy)
2. **What is the core transformation?** — Run the "So What?" chain 3 levels deep until you hit something emotional or financial
3. **What proof is available?** — Real testimonials, real client results, real numbers
4. **What format?** — Landing page / Email / Ad / Social / Website section

---

## Step 3: Write the Copy

**Headlines** — Generate 5 options across these angles:
- Outcome ("Get Found by Every Local Who Searches Your Service")
- Curiosity ("Why Most Tucson Plumbers Are Invisible to 80% of Local Searches")  
- Proof ("47 Local Businesses Ranked #1. Here's the System.")
- Contrarian ("Local SEO Agencies Won't Tell You This")
- Specific ("9-Mile Radius. Zero Blind Spots. Total Local Dominance.")

**Body** — Keep the reader moving. Vary sentence length. Bridge sections. Make pain concrete with real numbers.

**CTAs** — Name the reward, not the action:
- Instead of "Contact Us" → "Get Your Free Local Visibility Score"
- Instead of "Learn More" → "See How This Works in Your Market"
- Since booking URL unavailable: always include phone (+1 520-349-6378) or email (mark@markbishopmedia.com)

---

## Step 4: Score the Copy (7 Dimensions)

Rate each 1-10: Clarity, Specificity, Voice, Desire, Proof, Urgency, Flow
- 90%+ of 70 = ship it
- 80-89% = ship with tweaks  
- Below 70% = rewrite

Self-score honestly. Note what would fix any dimension below 7.

---

## Step 5: Humanizer Check

Read back the copy aloud (mentally). If it sounds like an AI wrote it:
- Replace any abstract nouns with concrete ones
- Add one specific local detail (city name, specific service, real number)
- Shorten every sentence by 20%
- Remove any sentence that starts with "We believe" or "Our mission"
