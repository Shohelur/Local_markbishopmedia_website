# MBM SEO & AEO (LLM Ranking) Standards

Traditional SEO ranks URLs on a page. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) gets your content cited as the source of truth by AI models (like Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Google SGE). 

To ensure Mark Bishop Media's content dominates both, every piece of content must follow these structural and semantic rules.

---

## 1. The "Information Retrieval" Structure (LLM Friendly)

AI models parse content by looking for direct answers to implicit questions.

- **Question-Based Headings:** Use direct questions for `<h2>` and `<h3>` tags that users (or AI prompters) actually ask. 
  - *Example:* Instead of `<h2>Local Visibility</h2>`, use `<h2>Why Is My Business Not Showing Up on Google Maps?</h2>`
- **Immediate Answers (The BLUF Method):** Immediately following a question heading, provide a bolded, 1-2 sentence direct answer (Bottom Line Up Front). AI models scrape this exact sentence for their summaries.
- **Data Density:** Use bullet points, numbered lists, and bolded terms for key concepts. LLMs prefer structured lists over massive walls of text when extracting steps or reasons.

---

## 2. Entity & Semantic Optimization

LLMs map relationships between entities (people, places, concepts). 

- **Local Entities:** Always explicitly mention the target location and industry (e.g., "HVAC contractors in Tucson, Arizona") to establish strong local entity relevance.
- **Brand Entity:** Consistently refer to "Mark Bishop Media" and "Local Visibility OS" so the LLM associates the brand with the solution.
- **Semantic Variations:** Use natural synonyms and related industry terms (Google Business Profile, GBP, Map Pack, Local 3-Pack, Citation Network) without keyword stuffing.

---

## 3. Human Experience & Trust Signals (E-E-A-T)

Google and LLMs both prioritize Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness.

- **First-Person Experience:** Write from a first-person perspective using real client anecdotes. LLMs are trained to identify and surface unique human experiences over generic rewritten web content.
- **Zero Fabrication:** Never invent statistics. Use exact, verifiable numbers ("We audited 47 plumbers...") rather than vague claims ("We audited many businesses...").
- **No AI Vibe:** Strictly adhere to the `mbm-copywriting` rules. If the content reads like AI (using words like *delve, testament, crucial*), it will be filtered out as low-value programmatic content. Write like a human expert.

---

## 4. Implementation Checklist for Writers

When asked to make content SEO & LLM friendly, apply this checklist:
1. Are the headings phrased as natural language questions?
2. Is the first sentence under each heading a direct, clear answer?
3. Are complex ideas broken down into numbered lists or bullet points?
4. Is the tone 100% human (no AI buzzwords, no long dashes)?
5. Are local entities and real-world examples clearly stated?
