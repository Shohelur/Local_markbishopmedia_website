# MBM Blog Content Master Plan
**Brand:** Mark Bishop Media — Local Visibility Operating System
**Target:** Local business owners (Plumbers, HVAC, Dentists, Contractors, Restaurants)
**Goal:** Rank on Google + LLM (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) within 10-30 days
**Voice:** Human storytelling, no corporate fluff, no long dashes, direct and confident

---

## Writing Rules (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

1. No long dashes. Use commas or short sentences instead.
2. No AI-sounding phrases like "In today's digital landscape."
3. Every blog starts with a human story or a question the business owner is asking RIGHT NOW.
4. Max 3 sentences per paragraph. Keep it scannable.
5. Every blog ends with a soft CTA: "Want to see exactly where your business stands? We will scan your listing for free."
6. No city-specific keywords. Industry-specific + problem-specific only.
7. Word count: 1,400 to 1,800 words. Depth without fluff.

---

## SEO Rules (Every Post)

- H1: Primary keyword (exact match or very close variation)
- H2s: Secondary keywords + People Also Ask questions
- First 100 words must contain the primary keyword naturally
- FAQ section at the bottom (minimum 4 Q&A pairs) for LLM citation
- Schema: Article + FAQ + Author (Mark Bishop)
- One internal link to the main site free audit section
- Meta title: Under 60 characters
- Meta description: Under 155 characters, includes primary keyword + CTA
