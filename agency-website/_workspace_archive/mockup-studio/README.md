# 🎨 Mark Bishop Media — Visual Mockup Studio

Welcome to your dedicated visual layout and design workspace!

## 📁 Studio Structure:
* **`01-hero-studio.html`**: The interactive visual canvas where you can drag, touch, draw, edit, and test layouts.
* **`saved-designs/`**: Directory where your exported layout specs and downloaded mockup PNGs are kept.

## 🚀 How to Use:
1. Double-click `01-hero-studio.html` to open it in your browser.
2. Choose a device: **Desktop**, **Tablet**, or **Mobile**.
3. Use your **mouse or laptop touchscreen / finger** to drag elements, pinch to resize, or draw arrows.
4. Try the **1-Click Layout Presets** (Golden Ratio, Centered, App Stack).
5. Click **"📸 DOWNLOAD MOCKUP PNG"** or **"📋 COPY SPECS FOR AI"** to share your design!
